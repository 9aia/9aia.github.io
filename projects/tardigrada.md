A tardigrade survives cryptobiosis — dormant, then alive anywhere. tardigrada
is the same for identity: one age keypair per machine or human, persisted in
a keyfile, shared as a public `age1…` recipient, and usable by every
encrypted store in the org — cuora vaults today, sealed packs and more
tomorrow.
