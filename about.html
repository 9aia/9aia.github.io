About

At Gaia, we are a small group of developers dedicated to exploring and developing minimal, open-source projects.

The Factory

We think of the stack as a factory, not a suite. Each tool is a machine: a small engine with one mechanism and a shipped preset. myco is the ledger on the floor, kuda the code memory, sturn the wiring, ci-gate the conveyor, sepia the shifts, peregrin the foreman, arca the safe.

None of them were designed as engines. They became them. Features kept pulling the same way until the mechanism stood alone and the domain was just configuration. That's the method: engines are grown from use, not planned upfront. Every new feature answers "engine or preset?" Machines stay small, replaceable, and owned.

And the factory is run by the workers it serves: agents are the operators, not the audience. Self-hosted, composable, yours.
